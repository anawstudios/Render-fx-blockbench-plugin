# Camera culling

<img width="763" height="602" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1126273b-fcd8-41cb-ba8f-5ca23e346eca" />

There are two types of culling here: one removes objects the camera cannot see—specifically, things outside the camera's field of view—while the other removes back-facing polygons to improve performance.

**To fix a lot of errors, I recommend enabling backface culling.**
